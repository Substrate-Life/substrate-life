## Discrepancies

### MAJOR — overbroad generality claim in §5a

`project-report.md:234` claims that **any** artificial-life system with obligate one-offspring-per-cycle reproduction has no income→fitness channel and that metabolic efficiency cannot be selected “no matter how the energy model is designed.”

That does not follow from `r=ln2/T`: income may alter `T`, probability of surviving to reproduction, or offspring establishment even when successful reproduction produces exactly one offspring. The report’s own §5c (`project-report.md:246`) recognizes reserve-dependent survival and offspring establishment. The claim should be confined to architectures where income affects neither timing nor survival once above a viability gate—not generalized to all obligate-single-offspring systems.

### MINOR — “20.0 boundary” relies on floating-point residue

The analytical spend is exactly 20.0, while the engine kills organisms at reserve `<=0`. Operationally, 20.0 survives only because binary floating arithmetic leaves `3.219646771412954e-15` after the READ tick (`offspring-first-extraction-threshold-summary.json:348–352`). In exact arithmetic it would reach zero and die before TRANSFORM.

Thus “20.0 reaches extraction” is accurate for the current Python-float implementation, but should be scoped as an implementation-measured boundary rather than a mathematically robust viability threshold.

### MINOR — machine-readable conditional artifact remains unreclassified

The prose reclassification is clear, but:

- `efficiency-conditional-third-bout-p1-summary.json:16` still states `"primary_clean_fecundity_gate": true`.
- It still publishes `"expected_rate_contrast_if_pass": 1/17`.
- `src/trace_efficiency_conditional_third_bout_p1.py:54–55` regenerates those fields without the post-hoc threshold caveat.

This preserves the original code-threshold result but is misleading if the JSON is consumed without the later prose. It should be explicitly treated as an archival, originally classified artifact—not current evidence of clean viable fecundity.

### MINOR — test invocation is working-directory dependent

From project root, `python3 -m unittest -v src/test_offspring_first_extraction_threshold.py` fails because the test imports `trace_offspring_first_extraction_threshold` as a top-level module. Running from `src/` passes both tests. This does not affect the scientific result but weakens routine regression discoverability.
