## 3. Exact concordance-rule power maps

Rule (repair prereg §5, source-frozen):
`ESTABLISHED_*` iff `#{D_i ≥ +floor} ≥ 18` or `#{D_i ≤ −floor} ≥ 18`
with `k_eff ≥ 16`; else `NO_ESTABLISHED_DIRECTION`. Under an
alternative in which each pair independently crosses the floor on one
side with per-pair probability `p_up`, power is the exact binomial tail
`P(Bin(k, p_up) ≥ ⌈3k/4⌉) = Σ_{i≥⌈3k/4⌉} C(k,i) pⁿ(1−p)^{k−i}`.

Exact tails (computed from integer arithmetic, no approximation):

| k | p=0.50 | p=0.60 | p=0.70 | p=0.75 | p=0.80 | p=0.90 |
|---|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| 24 | 0.01133 | 0.09596 | 0.38859 | 0.60741 | 0.81107 | 0.99254 |
| 48 | 0.00036 | 0.02190 | 0.27962 | 0.57676 | 0.85209 | 0.99934 |
| 96 | <10⁻⁵ | 0.00148 | 0.16938 | 0.55456 | 0.90903 | ≈1 |

Reading: at fixed per-pair success `p`, raising k does **not** rescue
power — below `p ≈ 0.75` it *reduces* it (0.389 → 0.280 → 0.169 across
k = 24/48/96 at p = 0.70). Concordance rules concentrate all their
power in per-pair signal beyond the floor.

## 5. Mean-based alternative rule at the same ecology

A new registration may carry any pre-declared statistic (§10 freezes
within a registration, not across them). Most powerful conventional
choice: paired mean test, n = 24, α = 0.05 two-sided. Required
displacement for 80% power: `μ* = (z_{0.975}+z_{0.80})·σ/√n =
2.80·5.7061/4.899 ≈ 3.26` lattice units. Two fatal contexts:

1. **Slope prior.** The expected displacement at the cross-sectional
   bound slope over the realistic-exclusion integral
   (`β·∫σ²_A dt = 8×10⁻⁵ · 9.1×10³ ≈ 0.73` units) yields noncentrality
   `0.73/(5.7061/√24) ≈ 0.63` ⇒ power ≈ 9%. The mean-rule is powered
   only for slopes `≥ 3.26/9.1×10³ ≈ 3.6×10⁻⁴` — **4.5× the bound**,
   i.e. the optimistic tail of everything ever measured here.
2. **Window scaling diverges once σ-growth is priced in.** Both arms'
   mutation-cloud means random-walk independently, so pairing cancels
   the shared demographic skeleton but not cloud drift; `σ_D ∝ √W`
   is the conservative model. Solving signal `β·455·T =
   2.80·σ₀·√(T/20)/√24` for turnovers T gives `T ≈ 401`,
   `W ≈ 48,000` ticks, estimated wall ≈ 55 h (linear scaling from the
   single recorded retained wall; see §6) — to test *exactly the
   bound* with zero margin and an extrapolated σ.

## 8. Conclusion (computed closure)

Every affordable route to bound-slope power fails on measured numbers:
the concordance rule needs per-pair mover probabilities > 0.75 that the
measured dispersion makes unreachable (whole-floor shift ⇒ 3.0% power);
replicate count cannot help below that threshold and hurts;
window extension either contradicts recorded ecology dynamics
(coexistence premise) or diverges in wall-time once cloud-drift noise
growth is priced in (≈ 30–55 h static-to-divergent under the recorded
regime); a mean-rule re-registration tests only slopes ≥ 4.5× the
cross-sectional bound; and the recruitment family is mechanically null
at the only ecologies characterised. The programme therefore closes
**because its two measured scales — effect (≲ 8×10⁻⁵ slope-analogue
cross-sectionally; redistribution bounded by best side 9/24, median
|D| = 4.55 lattice units) and noise (sd(D) = 5.71 lattice units per
20-turnover window) — are separated by more than any session-scale
instrument of this family can bridge.**

is made or implied that selection cannot act through the channel in
